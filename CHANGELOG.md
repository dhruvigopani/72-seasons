## 1.0.0 · 2026-11-20

First release.

- Small, medium and large Home Screen widgets.
- Inline and rectangular Lock Screen widgets.
- All 72 micro-seasons and 24 major seasons, with kanji, romaji and English names.
- 432 daily cues, six per micro-season.
- Large widget shows cues for the next three days, with a divider when a new season begins.
- Season colors that hold, then ease into the next over about a month.
- Film grain and a seasonal botanical branch that changes with each micro-season.
